# Support

**Need help right now?** If you're thinking about suicide or self-harm, call or text **988** (US). If you're in danger, call **911**. See [Safety](safety).

For anything about the app, use **Settings → Contact support** in Unreserved, or email **infoproductivityflows@gmail.com**. We usually answer within two business days.

## Common questions

**How does the free week work?**
Your first talk is free. After it, you can start a free week of Pro on the yearly plan. Nothing is charged during the week, and the app reminds you two days before it ends. If you don't cancel by then, the yearly plan starts and Apple charges you. To cancel, open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

**How do I cancel Pro?**
Open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Unreserved. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date. Apple handles refunds: [request one from Apple](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?**
In Unreserved, open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple account.

**How do I delete my data?**
Go to Progress, tap the gear, then Delete my data. That removes your history, transcripts and scores from your iPhone and from our servers.

**How do I get a copy of my data?**
Use Settings → Contact support and ask for a copy. The email includes your anonymous account ID, so we can find your data. See the [privacy policy](privacy).

**What counts as live minutes?**
Live talks and practice calls use minutes: Pro includes 450 a month, about 15 a day, and the free week includes 105. Drills and "Say it again" don't use minutes, because they run on your iPhone. Minutes reset at the start of each month.

**Why does my voice go to OpenAI?**
OpenAI's AI runs the live conversation. We don't keep recordings. See the [privacy policy](privacy).

**Are the callers real people?**
No. Every caller is a fictional character played by an AI. See [Safety](safety).

**I can't hear the whispers during a call.**
Whispers only play through headphones, so nobody nearby hears them. You can turn them on or off in Settings → Calls and reminders.

**Something went wrong. Do you know?**
If you leave Settings → Send crash reports on, the app tells us about crashes and failed features automatically, without anything you said. Contacting support still helps us fix it faster.

**Is this therapy?**
No. Unreserved is a practice tool, not a medical or mental-health service.

**Who can use Unreserved?**
Adults, 18 and older.
