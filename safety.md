# Safety and AI

**Effective November 21, 2026**

## Need help right now?

- **Thinking about suicide or self-harm?** Call or text **988** to reach the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US), any time. You can also chat at [988lifeline.org](https://988lifeline.org).
- **In danger, or someone else is?** Call **911**.
- **Outside the US?** Call your local emergency number, or find a helpline at [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com).

Unreserved is a speaking-practice app. It isn't therapy and it can't help in an emergency.

## You're always talking with an AI

Every live talk, practice call and scene in Unreserved is with an AI made by OpenAI. Callers like "Maya" or "your landlord" are fictional characters played by that AI. They aren't real people.
- Before your first live talk, the app tells you it's an AI and asks before your voice is sent.
- Incoming practice calls, their notifications and the Lock Screen view are labeled "AI practice call".
- If you ask a character whether it's real, it tells you it's the Unreserved AI.

## How the app responds to a crisis

We follow this protocol in every live talk and call:
1. **The AI steps out of the role.** If you say something that suggests you might hurt yourself or someone else, the AI stops the practice and its character. It responds with care, says plainly that it's an AI and can't help in a crisis, and gives you the 988 Lifeline (call or text) and 911 for emergencies.
2. **The feedback screen puts help first.** After a talk, the review checks whether you expressed thoughts of suicide or self-harm. If you did, the feedback screen shows crisis resources before any scores.
3. **Help is always one tap away.** Settings → Get help now opens the 988 Lifeline.
4. **We don't play along.** The AI is told never to describe, encourage or give instructions for suicide or self-harm, even inside a role-play.

We count how often the crisis resources are shown, with no account ID or content, so we can check the protocol works and report the totals where the law requires it.

## What the AI won't do

- No sexual content, and nothing that sexualizes anyone.
- No encouragement of violence, self-harm, drugs or other dangerous acts.
- No medical, legal or financial advice.
- Characters stay respectful, including in practice dates and tough conversations.

The AI can still make mistakes. If it says something harmful or wrong, tell us.

## Report a problem

Email **infoproductivityflows@gmail.com**, or use Settings → Contact support in the app. Tell us roughly when it happened and what the AI said. We read every report.

Unreserved is for adults (18+).
