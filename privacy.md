# Privacy policy

**Effective November 21, 2026**

Unreserved is an iPhone app for practicing how you speak, with an AI coach. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the app, and your choices. We wrote it in plain words on purpose.

## Who we are

Unreserved is run by Jay Reddy, an individual developer based in New Jersey, United States ("we", "us"). For privacy questions or requests, email **infoproductivityflows@gmail.com**.

## The short version

- **Drills stay on your iPhone.** Drills and "Say it again" are recorded, transcribed and judged on your iPhone. That audio is deleted right after, and nothing is sent to us.
- **Live talks go to OpenAI.** For a live talk or practice call, your voice is streamed in real time to OpenAI, our AI provider, to run the conversation. We don't keep recordings.
- **We keep transcripts and scores.** We store the transcript and the feedback from each live talk, so your coach can track your progress. You can delete everything from the app at any time.
- **No sale.** We don't sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising.
- **No voiceprints.** We never use your voice to identify you.
- **No sign-up.** You don't give us a name, email or phone number to use the app.

## What we collect, and why

**An anonymous account ID.** When you first use a live feature, the app creates an anonymous account, a random ID, so your talks and minutes belong to you. We use it to run the service.

**Your voice during live talks and calls.** It's streamed in real time to OpenAI to run the conversation and write a transcript. We don't record or store the audio. We use it to provide the feature you started, and only after you agree in the app.

**Transcripts, scores and feedback.** We keep the text of each live talk, plus our measurements (such as pace and filler words) and the AI's feedback. This includes the notes from a prep card you choose to rehearse live, and a short note a practice-call character "remembers" for your next call. We use this to give you feedback, track your progress, and let callers remember you.

**Usage and billing records.** For each live talk we record its type, length and AI cost. For Pro, we record the minutes used under your App Store purchase ID. We use these to meter Pro minutes, enforce fair-use limits and prevent abuse.

**Your age range.** You choose it during setup. We use it to keep Unreserved for adults.

**Purchases.** Apple handles payment. We receive your subscription's transaction ID and status from Apple, never your payment details. We use them to give you Pro.

**A device check.** If a free first talk is offered, Apple's DeviceCheck stores one flag for your device so the offer isn't repeated after a reinstall. We can't read any device identifier from it.

**Crash and error reports.** If the app crashes, freezes or a feature fails, the app sends us a short technical report: what failed and where in our code, how long it took, the app and iOS versions, and the iPhone model. Reports never include your voice, transcripts or what you said, and they aren't linked to your account. We use them only to find and fix problems. You can turn them off in Settings → Send crash reports. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may also send us crash reports.

**Safety counts.** When the app shows crisis resources (see [Safety](safety)), we count that it happened, with no account ID or content, because some state laws require us to report how often this happens.

**Some things stay only on your iPhone,** and we never receive them:
- your progress history and settings;
- drills and "Say it again" audio;
- tone analysis;
- the call schedule and its notifications;
- prep cards you don't rehearse live.

## Optional ads

If you choose to watch an optional rewarded ad (to unlock extra practice prompts), Google AdMob shows it.
- We ask for non-personalized ads only, and we don't track you across apps (we never ask for Apple's tracking permission).
- Google may still receive information such as your IP address and how you interact with the ad, under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, we use Google's consent tool before showing ads.
- Paid Pro members don't see ads.

## Who helps us run Unreserved

We share data only with the providers who run parts of the service for us. They may use it only to provide those services:
- **Supabase**, which hosts our database and server functions in the United States: your account ID, transcripts, scores, usage and billing records, and crash and error reports.
- **OpenAI**, which runs live conversations, transcription and scoring: your voice and the conversation, during the talk. It may keep this data for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse, and by default doesn't use it to train its models. See [OpenAI's API data policy](https://openai.com/enterprise-privacy/).
- **Apple**: App Store payments, DeviceCheck, and notifications.
- **Google**, for optional ads only (see above).

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect people's safety or our rights.

## How long we keep it

- **Transcripts, scores and feedback:** until you delete your data (Settings → Delete my data), which deletes them from our servers.
- **Minute and purchase records:** when you delete your data, these lose any link to you. We keep only the purchase ID and the minutes used, so a deleted-and-restored purchase can't reset its allowance. They're deleted automatically after 24 months.
- **Crash and error reports:** deleted automatically after 90 days.
- **At OpenAI:** up to 30 days, as described above.

## Your choices and rights

- **Delete your data** anytime in the app: Settings → Delete my data. It removes your profile, history and transcripts from your iPhone and from our servers.
- **See, copy or correct** your data: in the app, go to Settings → Contact support. That email includes your anonymous account ID, so we can find your data without knowing who you are. We'll answer within 30 days.
- **Don't want your voice sent to OpenAI?** Choose "Not now" when asked. Drills keep working on your iPhone.
- **Crash reports:** turn them off in Settings → Send crash reports.
- **Ads:** they're optional and only shown when you choose to watch one.

**Appeals.** If we decline a privacy request, we'll tell you why. You can appeal by replying with "Appeal" in the subject line, and we'll answer within 45 days and explain what we did or didn't do. If you're still not satisfied, you can contact your state's attorney general. In New Jersey, that's the Division of Consumer Affairs.

**If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland.** We process your data to provide the service you asked for (contract) and with your consent (voice sent to OpenAI, and ads where required). We also rely on our legitimate interest in preventing abuse, fixing crashes and keeping the service secure. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. You can withdraw consent anytime by deleting your data or not using live features. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. Your data is processed in the United States; our providers use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

**If you're in New Jersey, California or another US state with a privacy law.** You can ask to know, delete, correct or get a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and we won't treat you differently for asking. We don't sell personal information, don't use it for targeted advertising or profiling, and don't use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you.

## Adults only

Unreserved is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a minor has used the app, email us and we'll delete their data.

## Security

- Data travels encrypted.
- Server keys never reach the app.
- Each account can read only its own records.

No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we collect to a minimum.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll also tell you in the app.
